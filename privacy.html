Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: March 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how WeatherAPI Pro collects, uses, and shares information when you use our service.

Introduction

At WeatherAPI Pro, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. We believe in transparency and want you to understand how we handle your data. This policy applies to all users of our weather data services, including our website, API, and related applications.

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and improve user experience. By using WeatherAPI Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

Login Information: When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. This information is used to authenticate your account and provide access to our services.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our services, including API requests, features used, time spent on the platform, and diagnostic data. This helps us understand usage patterns and improve our services.

Location Data: To provide weather information for your area, we may collect location data that you provide voluntarily or through your device settings.

How We Use the Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

Service Improvement: We analyze usage data to understand how our services are used and identify areas for improvement. This helps us enhance performance, add new features, and provide a better user experience.

Personalization: Your information helps us customize content and provide relevant weather data based on your preferences and location.

Communication: We may use your email to send important updates, service announcements, and respond to your inquiries.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

• Encryption of data in transit and at rest
• Regular security assessments and audits
• Access controls and authentication protocols
• Secure storage infrastructure
• Employee training on data protection

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but continuously work to protect your data.

We may use third-party service providers to help us operate our business and services, such as hosting providers, analytics services, and payment processors. These providers have access to your information only to perform tasks on our behalf.
